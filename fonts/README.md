# Fonts

- `im_fell*.woff2` are **IM Fell English** (regular, italic and small caps), used for the names and footer. They are under the SIL Open
  Font License 1.1; the licence texts are `LICENSE-imfellenglish.txt` and `LICENSE-imfellenglishsc.txt`.
- `DejaVuSans.ttf` is the **DejaVu Sans** font (derived from Bitstream Vera), used as the fallback and for the sans-serif style. It is free to
  use, modify and redistribute under the Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license, which requires that the copyright and permission notice travel
  with copies. The full text is at <https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html> and in the upstream release archive (`LICENSE`).
  It is included here unmodified.
